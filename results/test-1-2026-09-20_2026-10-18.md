## What lost and why
Rationale comes from the desk notes (`dayN-*` closeouts, tickets and exec logs). Later prices are Yahoo daily closes.

- **Tight stops inside normal noise (4 of 5 losers).** NVDA's $223 stop was about 1.2% under entry and hit on the Sep 24 open; NVDA was back at $225.07 on Sep 25. SPY on Oct 8 was stopped $2.85 (0.37%) under entry and closed $773.93 that day and $778.57 the next. SPY's first trade was stopped at $759.90 near the day low of $758.79; it closed $763.99 the same day and $779.09 by Oct 6. XLE was stopped 47 minutes after entry. It was still weak through Oct 1 ($62.70) but ended the test at $68.24, +7.2% above the entry.
- **Process misses.** On Sep 30 the cleared SPY $760 and XLP $80.80 stops were never placed (day-8 closeout). XLP closed under its invalidation unprotected and was exited the next morning at $80.48, about $2.3 worse than the cleared stop. XLP ended the test at $87.62. On Oct 14 (day 18) the greenlit routine was never saved, so nothing was armed.
- **Under-deployment.** Peak exposure was about 14% and 10 of 20 sessions were flat all day, so the book could not earn much even when ideas were right. Pullback-only limit bands repeatedly went unfilled (days 1, 6, 12, 13, 16).
- **Picks were broad ETFs and defensives** (SPY, XLP, XLF, XLE) plus a single NVDA clip, with no single-name catalyst theses. The one winner (XLF, scaled out at targets) was the only trade with a pre-defined profit plan.
